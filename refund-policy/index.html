Digital-product refund policy

The exact scope of the Trip Cost Command Center 7-day fit promise.

Effective August 5, 2026

Our 7-day fit promise

If Trip Cost Command Center materially differs from its description or you cannot access it, request help within seven days of purchase using the in-product support form. We will restore access, correct the material issue, or refund the digital product.

What is not covered

A changed trip, a different provider price, or dissatisfaction with a third-party purchase does not establish that the digital system was misdescribed. Partner purchases follow the partner's refund rules.

Request details

Include the purchase email, product name, and a clear description of the issue. Approved refunds return through the original payment method; the financial institution controls when the credit appears.
