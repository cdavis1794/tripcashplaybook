What we publish

TripCash Playbook is an independent travel-cost and arrival-planning publication operated by Travel Tours, a Virginia-based sole proprietorship. We publish destination guides, provider-comparison checklists, and a self-service budget system. We are not a government, airport, airline, insurer, or booking provider.

Our method

  • Start with a real traveler decision: total cost, arrival route, baggage, connectivity, protection, or booking order.
  • Use official and first-party sources for laws, passenger rights, airport details, transportation rules, and product terms whenever possible.
  • Separate general decision guidance from affiliate inventory and never promise a fixed amount of savings.
  • Display review dates and revisit material claims when a guide is updated.

Corrections and independence

Affiliate relationships do not guarantee inclusion or favorable treatment. To flag a specific factual error, call (305) 250-1202 with the page and passage.
