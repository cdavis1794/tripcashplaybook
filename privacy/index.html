Privacy policy

What information the site receives and how it supports the service.

Effective August 5, 2026

Information we receive

We may receive information submitted through product feedback or support, including an email address and request details. Limited first-party analytics can record page views, an anonymous session identifier, product views, checkout starts, affiliate clicks, product-access events, and feedback. We do not request or store full payment-card numbers.

Use and providers

Information is used to operate the site, deliver products, restore access, respond to support, measure useful paths, prevent abuse, and meet accounting or legal obligations. Stripe processes digital-product payments. Affiliate and booking providers receive information when you visit or transact on their services. Netlify and related providers process technical data required to host and secure the site.

Your choices

To ask about information you submitted, call (305) 250-1202. Verification may be required before records are changed or deleted.
